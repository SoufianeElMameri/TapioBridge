Focus

Workflow problems worth turning into products.

The first product will not come from a vague idea about automation. It has to come from a repeated operational moment where missing information or missing functionality slows people down.

Signal we care about People already know the workaround.

The best clues are the manual steps teams have quietly accepted: switching tabs, searching records, copying details, asking another team, then returning to the original task.

Repeated The same interruption happens again and again.
Contextual The answer exists, but not where the work is happening.
Narrow The product can do one job clearly.
Reusable The same shape appears across multiple organisations.

Method

What must be clear before we build.

We look for real operational friction, clear user value, responsible access, and a focused product shape. The goal is simple: build software that makes work easier without forcing teams into another complicated system.

Product readiness filter

We compare each workflow against the practical conditions that decide whether it can become a focused product rather than an interesting one-off improvement.

  • Frequency How often does this task interrupt normal work?
  • Access Can the required information be retrieved responsibly?
  • Permission Who is allowed to use the information or functionality, and when?
  • Value Would removing the step save enough time or reduce enough risk?
  • Repeatability Does the same workflow exist outside one organisation?
